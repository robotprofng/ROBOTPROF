About RobotProf

Technology education should lead to real-world use.

RobotProf started with a simple idea: make technology education more accessible and help people learn by actually doing things.

Over time, our work has grown to include AI training for healthcare professionals. We now focus on helping people understand and apply AI, programming and data skills to problems in healthcare, research and beyond.

Healthcare professionals shouldn't have to choose between understanding healthcare and understanding technology. We want to help close that gap by making technical learning more practical, relevant and accessible.
